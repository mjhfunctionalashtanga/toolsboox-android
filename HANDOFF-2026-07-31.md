## Building

```
export DEVELOPER_DIR=/Users/michaeljoelhall/Downloads/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer
xcodebuild -project LedgerIpad.xcodeproj -scheme Ledger \
  -destination 'generic/platform=iOS' -allowProvisioningUpdates -derivedDataPath build-device build
```

applicable content" before a Swift file compiles) or `-target` (fails to resolve the Google package

## Do not "fix" these — they are decisions

- **Gram Picks is not a savable document.** An inbox you sort out of, not a shelf you keep.
- **Android prints a document's name as the page header; iOS prints the surface name.** iOS's Write
  page has always behaved that way, so doing it for Grid alone would be worse than for neither.
- **iOS's jot template still says "SKETCH NOTES"** — a word he never uses. Changing it alters the
  look of every jot page that already exists, which is why Android declined the same change.
- **Non-tag rhizome edges are never cleaned on delete.** That store's own policy: a rhizome broken
  at one point carries on along its other lines.
- **Backups are three separate jobs and should stay distinguishable:** names-and-structure (new
  today, both forks), the whole-ledger zip (the ink), and WebDAV sync. One button that sounds
  complete and isn't would be worse than three honest ones.

## Two lessons this session paid for

**Verify before building.** Reported diagnoses were wrong more often than right. The Listen lens was
never a `kind` bug — it was a client-side sieve held over a 50-row page. The almanac font was never a
PostScript-name fallback — all nine names verify against the shipping files; `Font.custom` just
substitutes silently. The "star all" starred nothing; it was a filter sitting in a row of verbs. And
Android already had half of what was about to be ported, twice. Check `HEAD`, not a working tree an
agent is mid-edit in.

**When both forks build one feature, pin the wire format in the brief.** Two agents built handwritten
titles in parallel and chose incompatible shapes, so a title written on the Boox could never reach
the iPad. Fixed in `07616852`. The names-backup work that followed pinned the schema up front and
both forks landed identical without coordinating.
